AWS penetration testing is scoped by accounts, identities, and exposed workloads, not IP ranges. AWS lets customers test a defined list of services without prior approval, but it prohibits flooding, DNS attacks, and takeovers, and it requires approval for command-and-control and simulated events. Budget follows two drivers: account and IAM complexity for the cloud control plane, and the number of internet-facing applications for workload testing.
